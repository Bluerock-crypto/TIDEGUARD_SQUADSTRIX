TIDEGUARD DATA NOTES
Collected: 26 Oct 2026 (pre-work, before 9:30 AM start)
Area: Guayaquil (Urdesa, city centre, south, north zones)

FILES
1. zones.json
   - 8 zones: name, lat, lon, elevation_m, dist_to_river_km, known_trouble_spot
   - Coordinates are approximate, checked on OpenStreetMap
   - Elevation: from Open-Meteo elevation API (real data, but coarse;
     4 of 8 zones are exactly 6 m, so it barely separates zones)
   - dist_to_river_km: distance to ONE fixed riverfront point, not to
     actual water. Rough proxy only.
   - known_trouble_spot: hand-labelled by us. Treat as the main
     vulnerability signal. Source of labels: our own judgement/research.

2. rain_2026-02-18.json
   - Hourly precipitation per zone for 18 Feb 2026 (Open-Meteo archive)
   - STATUS: REAL data, no nulls
   - Limit: gridded data, so zones share values. Only ~4 distinct rain
     profiles across the 8 zones. Do not claim street-level rain.
   - Peak: 15.3 mm/hr at 07:00, about 68 mm for the day at the highest zones.
     Heavy rain ran 01:00 to 07:00.

3. tide_test.json
   - Hourly sea level for 18 Feb 2026 (Open-Meteo marine)
   - STATUS: REAL model output, but offshore point in the Gulf
     (-2.7, -80.3), NOT the Guayaquil estuary
   - Behaves like a tide: highs at about 04:00 (1.15 m) and 16:00 (1.40 m),
     lows at about 10:00 and 23:00
   - Limit: estuary tide timing and height differ and probably lag this.
     Label as "coarse offshore tide" in the demo.
   - If it causes trouble: switch to simulated tide (2 highs and 2 lows a
     day, same function signature) and write "tide: simulated".

4. roads.graphml
   - OSM drivable road graph, 6 km radius around (-2.195, -79.90)
   - 13,438 nodes. Path check Urdesa to hospital area: True

BACKTEST (18 Feb 2026), what the data shows
- Rain and rising/high tide overlapped from about 01:00 to 07:00
- Worst rain hour (07:00) came after high tide, when the tide was falling
- Highest tide of the day (16:00, 1.40 m) had almost no rain, so it is a
  good "no alert" test case

KNOWN LIMITS (say these before a judge does)
- No ground truth for flooding on 18 Feb (no sensor data or flood reports yet)
- Backtest shows when the risk score WOULD flag danger, not that flooding
  was predicted or confirmed
- Rain is gridded, tide is offshore, elevation and river distance are proxies
- Zone coordinates are approximate

REAL vs SIMULATED
- Real: rain, elevation, road graph, offshore tide model
- Hand-made: zone list, known_trouble_spot labels
- Simulated (live demo only): sensor/report confirmation for road closures
